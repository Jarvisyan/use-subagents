# Vibe Paper

Use section-specific workflows to turn fixed research into clear, technically faithful paper content. Only the Method workflow is currently defined.

## Routing

For any Method or Methods task, including a constituent architecture, module, algorithm, objective, equation, pseudocode, or training/inference block, read [Method Writing](references/method.md) completely before planning or editing and follow it as the authoritative workflow.

Enter the smallest applicable stage: use Adversarial Method Plan for content or structure decisions, Adversarial Method Writing for an accepted block, and Whole-Chapter Check for a completed Method section. Reuse an accepted Plan when available; do not restart chapter planning for a local edit unless it changes the block's obligation, boundaries, or dependencies.

Do not generalize the Method workflow to unsupported paper sections.
